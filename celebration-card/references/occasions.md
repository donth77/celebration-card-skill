# Occasions: concepts, motifs, music, pitfalls

Starting points, not templates. The best concept still comes from the person's details: combine an occasion motif with something only they would recognise.

## Concept recipe

> **[occasion motif] × [their thing] → one transformation that happens over the song**

- Birthday × loves plants → a seed grows into a garden that spells their name on the final chorus.
- Retirement × pilot → a departure board flips to "RETIREMENT — NOW BOARDING". The flight path draws their career; it lands at "Destination: Anywhere".
- Anniversary × met at an observatory → a starfield where each memory is a star. The constellation draws their initials.
- Graduation × marine biology → an underwater scene; caps become bubbles rising to the surface into sunlight.

A transformation (empty → full, night → dawn, seed → bloom, scattered → assembled) gives the card an arc that maps onto the song's build.

## Birthday

- **Motifs:** cake and candles (blow-out moment), balloons (pop), confetti, gift box opening, the age in fireworks or number candles, "years in review".
- **Music:** Happy Birthday (synth, PD) as music box, lo-fi or big band; a custom Suno song with the name; upbeat pop.
- **Interactions:** blow candles (mic/tap), pop balloons, make a wish (type it, it floats away), shake for confetti.
- **Copy:** specific memories, a running joke, a wish for the year ahead. Skip "another trip around the sun".
- **Pitfalls:** age teasing only if the sender wants it. Spell out the age in big type only if they're proud of it (ask on milestones for adults).

## Milestone birthdays

| Age | Idea |
|---|---|
| 18 / 21 | freedom and future: open roads, city lights, party energy |
| 30 / 40 | "best year yet", a decade-in-review montage |
| 50 / 60 | a golden palette, "the story so far", decade chapters with era-styled typography (born 1976 → '70s type and colours) |
| 70+ | grandkids' messages, family tree, gentle orchestral or classic jazz |
| 100 | a century timeline, with historic events of their birth year as delightful context |

Number candles in 3D (`TextGeometry`, see wish.js) and the age forming in fireworks or particles are crowd-pleasers.

## Kids' birthday

- Bright, bouncy, big shapes, a mascot animal or their favourite thing (dinosaurs, space, unicorns: draw it simply, don't use trademarked characters).
- Music: chiptune, marimba, ukulele. Synth Twinkle/Happy Birthday variations.
- Interactions: tap everything; every tap makes a sound and a burst. Short (≈60 s).
- Assume a parent is reading the words to them. Few words, big type.

## Anniversary

Traditional gifts by year (a nice motif or palette):

| Year | Material | Year | Material |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | paper | 13 | lace |
| 2 | cotton | 14 | ivory |
| 3 | leather | 15 | crystal |
| 4 | fruit / flowers (linen) | 20 | china |
| 5 | wood | 25 | silver |
| 6 | iron (sugar) | 30 | pearl |
| 7 | wool / copper | 35 | coral (jade) |
| 8 | bronze | 40 | ruby |
| 9 | pottery | 45 | sapphire |
| 10 | tin / aluminium | 50 | gold |
| 11 | steel | 55 | emerald |
| 12 | silk / linen | 60 | diamond |
| | | 70 | platinum |

- **Motifs:**
  - two lights orbiting each other
  - a map line connecting the places they've lived and travelled
  - a constellation of memories
  - "then & now" photo pairs
  - a counter of days together
  - the wedding song reimagined (PD only, or synthesized)
- **Music:** Canon in D (synth), a tender original, or a Suno duet with both names.
- **Copy:** the story of *them*, with small specific moments. From a couple to each other, or from the kids.

## Wedding / engagement

- Elegant type (Cormorant, Playfair + a script), florals, rings, two names intertwining, the date as typography.
- Music: Canon, Minuet in G, strings and harp (synth), or a custom song.
- A group card from guests suits a "guest book" keepsake wall.

## Retirement

- **Motifs:** the journey (map or timeline of a career), "the next chapter" (pages turning), sunset → sunrise, open road, departure board, their hobby taking over (golf course, garden, fishing lake, sailboat).
- **Music:** feel-good classic soul/rock (Suno), a triumphant fanfare (synth), Auld Lang Syne (synth) for a fond goodbye.
- **Group cards are common:**
  - colleagues' messages as lanterns or paper planes
  - a credits roll of names
  - the numbers: years served, projects shipped, coffees consumed
- **Copy:** celebrate the contribution and what's ahead. Avoid "old" jokes unless asked. Mention what they'll be missed for, concretely.

## Farewell / moving / new job

- Suitcase, postcards, paper planes, a map with the new city glowing, "see you soon" not "goodbye".
- The team's messages as postcards from everyone.
- Music: upbeat indie or folk, Auld Lang Syne variations, a travel-montage feel.

## Promotion / achievement

- A rocket launch, a level-up (video-game UI), a trophy in a spotlight, a curtain-call stage, a "breaking news" chyron.
- Music: fanfare (synth example), a celebratory anthem.
- Keep it short and punchy (45–75 s).

## Graduation

- Caps tossed (3D, physics-y arcs that never intersect), a diploma unrolling, school colours, "Class of 2026", paths branching into the future.
- Music: an original fanfare, Ode to Joy, a pop anthem. Pomp and Circumstance is PD in the US, but don't synthesize it from memory unless you're sure of every note.
- Copy: pride, the journey, specific late nights. Avoid generic "the world is your oyster".

## New baby / baby shower

- Soft palettes, the moon and stars, clouds, a mobile turning slowly, the baby's name in wooden blocks, a lullaby (synth Brahms-style original or Twinkle).
- Gentle volume, slow motion, no loud booms or bright flashes. New parents are tired, and the baby may be asleep nearby.
- Details: birth date, weight and time as a "birth announcement" typographic moment.

## Thank you / appreciation

- Teachers, nurses, mentors, volunteers. Hearts or gratitude jars filling up, notes from many people, "you made a difference" with concrete examples.
- Warm, sincere, unhurried. A credits roll of everyone they helped is powerful.

## Get well

- Gentle: sunshine through a window, a garden, a cozy blanket of messages. Uplifting but calm music, nothing manic.
- Don't joke about the illness unless the sender knows them well and asked for it. Keep it short. Avoid "get well soon" pressure for long-term conditions ("thinking of you" is safer).

## Memorial / celebration of life

- Handle with real care, and be gentle in conversation too; the person asking may be grieving.
- **Look:** calm palette, soft light, candles, stars, water, a garden at dusk. **No confetti, no fireworks, no party sounds.**
- **Pace:** slower, with room to pause; keep the mute and replay controls visible.
- **Music:** reflective. Satie-like piano or strings (synth original, or a PD/CC0 recording), or a song that was theirs, if the user has it.
- **Content:** photos through the years, their own words if available, messages from family. The keepsake becomes a guestbook-like wall.

## Holidays & cultural celebrations

Be culturally accurate, and ask when unsure:
| Celebration | Motifs |
|---|---|
| Lunar New Year | red and gold, lanterns, the zodiac animal of the year, wishes for prosperity |
| Diwali | diyas (oil lamps), rangoli patterns, warm golden light. Fireworks are culturally fitting here |
| Hanukkah | menorah candles lit one by one (8 + shamash), blue/white/silver, dreidels |
| Eid | crescent moon and star, lanterns, warm night palette |
| Christmas / winter holidays | snow, pine, warm windows, Jingle Bells (synth, PD) |
| New Year | countdown to midnight synced to the song, fireworks, Auld Lang Syne |
| Quinceañera, Bar/Bat Mitzvah, other coming-of-age celebrations | follow the family's traditions and colours; ask |

## Pets

- Birthdays and "gotcha days": paw prints, treats raining as confetti, the pet as the star with photos. Funny copy written in the pet's voice works well.
- Music: playful marimba or ukulele. Cut-out photo stickers of the pet bouncing on the beat (use a simple CSS mask if the photo has a plain background).
